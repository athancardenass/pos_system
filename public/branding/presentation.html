Final design

Market Canopy

An iconic scalloped supermarket storefront canopy fused with a rolling checkout cart.

  • Unmistakable grocery storefront heritage paired with modern checkout mobility.
  • Calibrated palette of Slate Gray (#1E293B) and Peach Fuzz (#FFBE98) for hardware authority and organic warmth.
  • Engineered for high readability from 16px desktop browser tabs to physical store signage.
