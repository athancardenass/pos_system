# Supermarket POS System — Logo Guidelines & Brand Kit

## 1. The Logo
- **Concept**: **Market Canopy** — Merges an authentic scalloped supermarket storefront awning (heritage, hospitality, retail entrance) with a rolling checkout cart chassis (speed, mobility, modern POS operations).
- **Core Versions**:
  - `market-canopy-symbol.svg` (Primary standalone symbol)
  - `market-canopy-horizontal.svg` (Primary horizontal lockup with wordmark & subline)
  - `market-canopy-stacked.svg` (Vertical badge / signage lockup)
  - `market-canopy-app-icon.svg` (Squircle container app icon for mobile & desktop)
  - `market-canopy-black.svg` & `market-canopy-white.svg` (Monochrome pure black and inverted white)
  - `market-canopy-mono-1e293b.svg` & `market-canopy-mono-ffbe98.svg` (Single brand-color masters)
  - `favicon.ico`, `favicon.svg`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png` (Complete web & PWA icon suite)

## 2. Color Palette
| Color Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Slate Gray** | `#1E293B` | `rgb(30, 41, 59)` | `C81 M70 Y48 K48` | Primary Structural Tone (Awning bar, chassis, wheels, typography) |
| **Peach Fuzz** | `#FFBE98` | `rgb(255, 190, 152)` | `C0 M30 Y41 K0` | Fresh Retail Accent (Awning panels, wheel hubs, status tags) |
| **Surface White** | `#FFFFFF` | `rgb(255, 255, 255)` | `C0 M0 Y0 K0` | Background canvas & reversed knockout elements |

## 3. Clear Space & Minimum Sizes
- **Clear Space**: Keep a minimum clear breathing margin of **1× the wheel radius** (`18px` proportional unit) around the mark on all sides.
- **Minimum Reproduction Sizes**:
  - Horizontal lockup: `120px` wide on screen / `30mm` in print.
  - Standalone symbol: `16px` on screen (use `favicon-16.png` or `favicon.svg`) / `6mm` in print.

## 4. Approved Pairings
- **Full-Color on White / Light Surface**: Standard display for UI cards, receipts, and headers.
- **Inverted Full-Color on Slate Gray (`#1E293B`)**: White structural bars with Peach Fuzz panels on dark terminals.
- **Pure Black on White**: 1-bit thermal receipt printers and monochrome laser documents.
- **Pure White on Dark / Video / Photography**: High-contrast apparel, storefront signboards, or dark mode shells.

## 5. Don'ts (Misuse Rules)
- Do NOT alter relative proportions between the awning canopy and the rolling cart wheels.
- Do NOT stretch, condense, skew, or rotate the mark.
- Do NOT substitute uncalibrated neon greens or harsh reds for the official Slate Gray and Peach Fuzz palette.
- Do NOT place the colored logo over competing, high-noise photographic backgrounds without a solid container tile.
